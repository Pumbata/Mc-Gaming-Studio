Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback about Binder Sort or Endless Dusk.

Email mcgamingstudio26@gmail.com. Please include the game name, your phone model and Android version, and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Progress is stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data erases it, because we don't run accounts or cloud saves.

How do I remove ads? When available, a "Remove ads" purchase in the game turns off ads shown between levels. Optional "watch an ad for a reward" videos stay available but are never required.

A purchase didn't arrive. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Restart the game first; if it's still missing, email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).

How do I delete my data? See Your choices in our privacy policy.
