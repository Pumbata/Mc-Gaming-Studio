Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Applies to Binder Sort, Endless Dusk and any other game published by Mc Gaming Studio on Google Play.

Mc Gaming Studio ("we", "us") makes mobile games. This policy explains what information our games collect, why, and the choices you have. In short: we don't ask for your name, email or any account, and your game progress stays on your phone. Our games show ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.

We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location, or other personal files, and our games don't require you to create an account.

Notifications

Binder Sort can remind you once a day that the daily challenge is ready. The reminder is off until you turn it on in the game's Settings, it's scheduled entirely on your phone, and you can turn it off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings at any time.

Advertising

Our games use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google's use of this information is governed by its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later from the game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices").

Some optional ads let you earn a reward, like extra undos. Watching them is always your choice.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game, or go to Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data. Because progress is only on your device, this removes it completely.
  • Data held by Google: manage it at myaccount.google.com.
  • Questions or requests: email mcgamingstudio26@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states, the EU or the UK), you may have rights to access, delete or correct personal information, or to opt out of its use for targeted advertising. We'll help with any request we can, and point you to Google for data it holds.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security and retention

Game progress never leaves your device. Emails are kept only as long as needed to help you. Information collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

Changes

If our games start collecting new kinds of information (for example, adding analytics or cloud saves), we'll update this page and the effective date before the change goes live.
