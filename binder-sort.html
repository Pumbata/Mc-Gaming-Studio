Binder Sort

A cozy card-sorting puzzle. Sort shuffled trading cards into piles by element, seal every pile, and file the creatures you win into your binder.

Coming soon to Google Play

What's inside

  • Hundreds of levels across nine worlds, with boss levels, timed rush levels and gentle breathers in between.
  • New twists as you go: face-down cards, taped piles that open once you seal a set, and a holo pocket that holds one card.
  • 81 original creature cards in nine sets: fire foxes, lantern fish, moon owls, crystal sprites, storm wolves and more.
  • Booster packs, dust and crafting: open packs with the coins you earn and craft the cards you're missing. Packs are never sold for real money.
  • Equip a card for a bonus every level, play the daily challenge, and keep your login streak going.
  • Plays offline. Your progress stays on your phone. No account needed.
